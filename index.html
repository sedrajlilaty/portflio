About me

A quick summary of who I am and how I work.

Frontend is where I like to work, because it's the layer people actually feel — every state change, every loading skeleton, every RTL flip has to be right or the whole product feels broken.

I'm a fourth-year Computer Engineering student at Damascus University, currently ranked 9th out of roughly 1,300 students in my cohort — but most of what I know about shipping real software came from building outside the classroom: React dashboards, Flutter apps talking to real Laravel and Node APIs, and interfaces that hold up in both Arabic and English.

I've already taken my first real steps into the backend too — building a Node.js shop end-to-end, with authentication, cart, checkout, and real payment integration — and I'm continuing that path toward full-stack, on top of leading frontend on Ataa's React/Flutter/Laravel system.
